# O3 version C: trained visibility estimator (v2)

Generated by `python -m wad.vis_eval --version 2`. Pre-registration: `PREREG_visibility.md` (three dated amendments, all made before any real-image prediction). Training: synthetic degradations of clear-day BDD100K train frames; evaluation: BDD100K val and DAWN, never seen in training or model selection. Detector for the threshold policies: YOLOv8x @1280, grouped vehicles (DAWN clean labels); α = 0.2, 200 calibration/test splits.

Training: 3989 train / 462 validation base frames (49 dark, mis-tagged frames excluded); best epoch 19; synthetic validation MAE (log10): log_mor 0.069, log_k 0.039, log_veff 0.069, map 0.104.

Development check (BDD100K train frames, not evaluation data): 400 night vs 400 day frames, ROC AUC of the light level 0.868, median k night 0.94 vs day 1.01. Post hoc: run after the v2 evaluation, as the baseline for version 3.

By construction V_eff ≤ 2000 m (V_light = 2000 m at k = 1). The top fixed band (≥ 1000 m) holds daylight without a significant veil or spray, and any image read as no darker than k = 0.25 (dusk level); it is not 'no darkness'.
