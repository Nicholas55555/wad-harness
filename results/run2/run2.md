**What this changes for the project.** Night (and, on DAWN, fog) is where one threshold breaks the miss budget and per-bin calibration earns its false positives; daytime rain also costs recall per object, but on BDD100K a clear-calibrated threshold still met α = 0.2 in the rain bin as sampled. α = 0.1 was certifiable in every DAWN bin only with YOLOv8x @640 and the grouped loss, a variant singled out after the results; the pre-registered primary reaches α = 0.2 everywhere. The hazard estimator (O3) should therefore separate visibility (night, fog) first.
