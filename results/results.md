## 4. Phase 4: conformal risk control per weather bin

Rule: τ̂ = max{τ on the 200-point grid : n/(n+1)·R̂(τ) + 1/(n+1) ≤ α} with R̂ the mean per-image miss fraction on the calibration half; τ̂ = 0.001 and *infeasible* when no grid point qualifies. 10 splits (seeds 0–9), each half of every weather bin, near-duplicate groups kept together. Test metrics are on the held-out half: image loss = mean per-image miss fraction (the controlled quantity), FPPI = false positives per test image, exceed = fraction of splits whose test image loss is above α. Values are mean ± sd over splits; n_cal / n_test are images per split.

**Reading.** At α = 0.1, oracle-bin fits are infeasible in Fog 100%, Rain 100%, Sand 100%, Snow 80% of splits: even at the 0.001 floor the mean per-image miss fraction is 0.118–0.218 across bins, so α = 0.1 cannot be certified in most splits of any bin, and both CRC policies collapse to (or next to) the floor at 38–74 FPPI. In the 2 splits where it was certified (Snow seed 0, Snow seed 2, τ̂ just above the floor), the held-out loss was 0.145, 0.146, above α: a marginal guarantee does not promise any single split. The smallest budget the rule can certify (calibration halves, mean over splits) is Fog 0.229, Rain 0.154, Sand 0.192, Snow 0.117 (pooled 0.175). At α = 0.2, oracle-bin CRC is feasible in Rain, Sand, Snow (test loss Rain 0.191, Sand 0.195, Snow 0.215; τ̂ Rain 0.0154, Sand 0.0021, Snow 0.0415) and infeasible in Fog. The 10-split means carry split noise; section 6.1 repeats this with 200 splits.

## 8. Caveats

- **Infeasible bins.** Oracle-bin fits with no feasible threshold (fraction of splits): α=0.1 Fog 1.0, α=0.1 Rain 1.0, α=0.1 Sand 1.0, α=0.1 Snow 0.8, α=0.2 Fog 1.0, α=0.2 Sand 0.2. There τ̂ is the 0.001 floor and the conformal guarantee does not hold: the loss does not vanish at the floor.
- **The GO verdict is fragile for person** (bootstrap P(GO) = 0.64): the worst person bin is a near tie between sand and fog (max recall 79.9% vs 80.7%), and fog's margin is 14.7 points against the 15-point rule.
- **Duplicates.** 22 exact duplicates excluded; 133 near-duplicate pairs kept but never split across halves; 11 pairs carry different weather labels.
- **Zero-detection images:** 3 (fog/haze-012, fog/haze-033, fog/mist-133); 4 images reach max_det = 300, which may cut low-score true positives there (NOTES.md).
- **Small samples** (fewer than 30 boxes): fog 16 bicycle, fog 22 motorcycle, rain 28 person, rain 0 bicycle, rain 4 motorcycle, rain 13 bus, sand 9 bicycle, snow 1 bicycle, snow 1 motorcycle, snow 22 bus; per-class results for these rest on a handful of images per bin.
- **The guarantee is marginal and per bin**, an expectation over calibration draws; a split-half design on a fixed pool makes calibration and test halves negatively correlated, so single splits swing around α.
- **Detector input resolution.** DAWN images are mostly 1280 px wide and are downscaled to 640; most never-detected objects are small at that scale (section 6.2).
