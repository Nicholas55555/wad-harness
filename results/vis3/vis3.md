# O3 version C: trained visibility estimator (v3)

Generated by `python -m wad.vis_eval --version 3`. Pre-registration: `PREREG_visibility.md`, section 'Version 3'. Not blind: designed after v2's results (v2 read real night as daylight); development used only BDD100K train night frames. v2 is shown alongside. Training: synthetic degradations of clear-day BDD100K train frames; evaluation: BDD100K val and DAWN, never seen in training or model selection. Detector for the threshold policies: YOLOv8x @1280, grouped vehicles (DAWN clean labels); α = 0.2, 200 calibration/test splits.

Training: 3989 train / 462 validation base frames (49 dark, mis-tagged frames excluded); best epoch 19; synthetic validation MAE (log10): log_mor 0.084, log_k 0.049, log_veff 0.071, map 0.121.

Development check (BDD100K train frames, not evaluation data): 400 night vs 400 day frames, ROC AUC of the light level 0.991, median k night 0.32 vs day 1.01. The same night frames were used to tune the night rendering, so this is not an independent check.

By construction V_eff ≤ 2000 m (V_light = 2000 m at k = 1). The top fixed band (≥ 1000 m) holds daylight without a significant veil or spray, and any image read as no darker than k = 0.25 (dusk level); it is not 'no darkness'.
