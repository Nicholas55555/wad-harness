Trade-off Dial for YOLOv8

Instead of a fixed confidence cut-off, set the trade-off you want: how many false alarms you accept to avoid one missed object (λ), or how many times more recall counts than precision (β, as in Fβ). Then choose how it is applied: one confidence threshold for every box, or a cut on each detection's chance of being real. Every setting was fitted on BDD100K train images; here it is graded on dashcam frames it never saw.
